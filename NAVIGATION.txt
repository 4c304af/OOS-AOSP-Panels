Bluetooth navigation in 0.6.33
The module checks the BluetoothDetailsContentManager against the active contentManager in its own BluetoothDetailsContentViewModel. The original ActivityStarter receives the original View.
See All originally sends com.android.settings.PREVIOUSLY_CONNECTED_DEVICE. In this ROM the OEM Bluetooth list is android.settings.BLUETOOTH_SETTINGS in com.oplus.wirelesssettings.
The standard device gear sends com.android.settings.BLUETOOTH_DEVICE_DETAIL_SETTINGS with device_address inside :settings:show_fragment_args. Wireless Settings exports wireless.settings.DEVICE_PROFILES_SETTINGS with com.oplus.permission.safe.SECURITY, granted to this SystemUI. Its SettingsActivity maps this action to its own profile fragment, which reads the BluetoothDevice parcelable under device in its arguments. The module converts only the selected address. Other device-specific companion actions and pairing follow the original flow.
The OplusBluetoothTile inherits BluetoothTile.getLongClickIntent(). When the combined grid is visible, its Bluetooth settings intent is restricted to the OOS list handler. The tile keeps its own launch and keyguard checks.
